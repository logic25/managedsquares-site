Our approach

Owners carry the risk. Someone should work only for them.

Most people on a project are paid by the size of the job. An owner's representative is paid to protect the owner's budget, schedule and decisions. That is the whole job, whether it's one renovation or a development that takes years.

We work in stages, so each step answers one question before the next, larger check is written.

How an engagement runs

1

Study

Does it work on paper? A small, fixed budget answers the first question before anything else is spent.

2

Decide

Ground truth, partners and financing lined up. The owner decides with real numbers in hand.

3

Build

Contract, construction and closeout, managed for the owner from start to finish.
